# Puzzle Cove platform

The Puzzles door opens `/cove`. `CoveScreen` consumes real `cove: true` Challenge registrations; it does not register games or manufacture bank entries. School shelves and suggestions are unchanged.

## 4.0 Clean (docs/LITTLE_ISLAND_4_DESIGN.md, Puzzles (Cove))

- **Home** (`CoveHome`): `←`, "Puzzles", the compact profile pill (`PeoplePicker`, "Who's playing?" with a separate "Puzzles only" row), then a Continue strip (paused puzzles and family pins with the sender's face), Favorites, the Today tile (the existing `tideTable` Medium pick) and five sections of picture tiles. Tap opens a start sheet; long-press favorites. No counts, band words or paragraphs.
- **Shelf** (`shelf.ts`): one declarative `SECTIONS` list. It shapes the tile grid only. Continue, pins, `/cove?game=`, `/cove?person=&game=` and Choose a puzzle resolve against every registered game, so deferred games (Cage Math, Greater-Than Grid, Skyline, Pearl Loop, …) keep working. A listed game that is not registered yet is left out until its PR lands. Reef Sweep is a shortcut to `/challenge?game=reef-sweep` as the current learner and is never a Cove entry. School originals open through the map's own route with the School save.
- **Difficulty words**: tiers 1–6 show as `Easy · Medium · Hard · Expert · Master · Master+` with a size tag from the bank facts. The Cove local `band` preference keeps its numbers.
- **Start sheet** (`StartSheet`): picture, name, difficulty pills for the tiers the game has (a size range such as `5×5–6×6` when banks differ), On my own / Play together (family faces), a favorite star, and one coral `Play`, or `Continue` (the exact saved attempt, shown with its own saved difficulty and partner) over the choices "For a new puzzle" and a quiet `New puzzle` (which asks first, in the round). Opening, failure and "No other puzzle here" feedback (with Choose a puzzle) shows in the sheet; closing it or changing a choice cancels a deal still opening.
- **Board** (`CoveRound`): one `Hud` row (`←` save and leave, name and exact difficulty such as `Easy 6×6`, Undo, Hint, `•••`). The HUD never sends a raw reveal or edit. A hosted board hands over its own commands through `BoardHud` (`boardHud.ts`): Hint is the board's first staged step (Sudoku and its cousins, Bridges: Look; Solitaire: Hint → Think → One step), Undo and Start over carry the board's own cleanup, and the board leaves its duplicate buttons out and shows the plain title (`Hard · 9×9`). A board that does not hand them over (Loop, Pearl Loop, Word Search) keeps its own and the HUD shows neither; only a frame-help game gets `controls.help()`, which records help and changes nothing. More (`RoundMore`, the kit's undimmed small sheet) offers Mistakes only where the board reads it live (`honorsMistakes`), Restart this puzzle as the same attempt's engine reset (help audit, id, ordinal, partner and options kept; a full Solitaire log restarts the deal the documented way), New puzzle, Choose a puzzle, Send to… with refusal feedback in place, Roles and How to play. While a finish waits for its partner choice, the actions that would replace the round stay disabled. The board shrinks to the room above More inside the screen's measured height, never below 280 pt. Finish is one card with `Next puzzle` and `Puzzles`, with faces for "Solved together with…".
- **Choose a puzzle** (`PuzzleBrowser`, `PackedBrowser`, `ChooseGrid`): a large dimmed sheet of its own (title focus, Close, swipe, Escape, VoiceOver escape) over the mounted round, so closing it returns to exactly what was there, a finished card included; More steps aside while it is open and returns when it closes. A numbered grid for one difficulty with finished ticks, a set row when a difficulty has several banks, and a small All / Finished / Mixed practice filter. Hidden puzzles never appear, and a failed hidden-row lookup offers nothing; a paused hidden puzzle still resumes from Continue.
- **Grown-ups** (`CoveGrownUps`): reader level, fill notes and hiding stay behind the multiplication gate, reached from More, until the app-wide Grown-ups sheet lands.
- **New deals** (`fresh` in `CoveScreen`): a puzzle at the chosen difficulty that is neither hidden nor the one being replaced, trying the current bank and then every other bank at that difficulty. Every card a chooser returns is checked again, so a chooser's own fallback never reopens a hidden or identical puzzle; with nothing left the sheet says "No other puzzle here" and offers Choose a puzzle rather than switching difficulty. Today uses the same path. Continue is separate and always reopens the exact saved ref.
- **Continue links**: `/cove?game=<id>` opens the start sheet; `/cove?game=<id>&resume=1` (the map's Continue, the old picker's Continue and the named Resume shortcut) reopens that game's paused attempt in one tap, falling back to the sheet when nothing is paused. The Continue strip shows four cards and a `See all (N)` sheet with every paused puzzle and pin, deferred games and pins to hidden puzzles included.
- **Reef Sweep**: the tile opens its Challenge (`/challenge?game=reef-sweep`) per the design spec. The Hall's Reef Sweep is a different save (`/board?game=reef-sweep`); converging the two is the Hall slice's decision.

A game opens its owner's unfinished attempt, otherwise a puzzle in their chosen band. `CoveRound` uses Store's `challengeOpen` and `challengeSave` for exact content admission, autosave, owner/epoch/revision guards, preservation and atomic Together completion. Together is chosen before launch or explicitly confirmed with one tap at a solo finish; an existing Together attempt keeps its original partner. A finish awaiting confirmation says it has not saved yet. Launch and restore return to the current learner. Leaving the route clears the temporary Cove-only identity through the same epoch-guarded Store operation as the Map button. A retained route cannot start an attempt while it is out of focus. The round never calculates completion itself. Readiness for the generated School bridge remains a lead-controlled registration decision.

`presentCove(entry, presentation)` adds adapter-specific band descriptions, techniques, template IDs, optional reader forms and permitted remix launch functions. It adds no refs, counts or approvals. All six tiers remain open. The mixed-practice selector only reuses solo-solved registered refs (codes 2, 5 and 6), requires multiple techniques/four templates, and caps any template at 25%. Remixes keep the same ref and must pass the adapter's existing launch codec. No remix is inferred from mirrored art.

Cove-only people and puzzle progress live in the existing validated save envelope. Device-local preferences, favorites, hidden puzzle hashes and quiet pins and a non-active last-person resume hint use `local.ts` with SQLite KV on native and localStorage on web. They are explicitly outside the app backup. Invalid/future preference bytes are preserved before an explicit fresh-choice reset. Failed writes do not publish. Durable writes notify retained screens; removed-owner choices are pruned. There are no notifications or date-play records. Pins never delete an older pin to make room. Removing a Cove person remains the existing Store's grown-up/preserved-save operation; this slice adds creation and selection.

Packed games (an entry with `packed` bands and no inline cards) appear by bank. Play, Today or Next puzzle deals the next entry through `packed.choose`; `PackedBrowser` numbers each bank's entries and opens one exactly; a saved attempt is prepared through `packed.prepare` before its session opens. A puzzle hidden on this device is stored by hash. For packed games the registration's `hiddenRows` finds it in its bank (reading that bank only when something is hidden), so the chooser grid, the Today pick and every deal (`dealPacked`, `chooseVisible`) skip it. A saved attempt on a hidden puzzle still continues from Continue.

A solo finish saves at once when nobody else could be credited. With other people present, the finish card offers Together; leaving it with `←` saves the solo finish first.

The shared grid is in `src/puzzles/grid`:

- `GridShell` renders memoized RN cells, one static SVG path layer and a thin composited cursor. Candidate notes are one text layout per cell. Only changed cells update; selection updates two cells. Large non-digit boards start enlarged when needed. Digits use cell-first or number-first large-pad entry. Zoomed boards pan with two fingers; one finger selects or paints an axis-locked line. A focused overlay handles web keyboard input; an iOS TextInput accepts typed symbols/Backspace without opening the software keyboard, with ordinary cell accessibility labels still present. Keyboard selection reveals offscreen cells.
- `model.ts` owns nearest-cell hit tests and an unused nearest-edge geometry helper (edge input remains G2 open work), axis lock/interpolation, a provisional 160 ms repeat-touch filter, notes, whole-stroke undo/redo, and bounded packed drafts. `encodeGrid` can discard oldest undo working memory to meet its byte budget; it never drops the current board or rejects an ordinary edit because a history buffer filled. Adapters must reserve wrapper and audit bytes within the Store's 4,096-byte session limit.
- `checkpoint.ts` retains Check/help/reset audit events outside undo, compacts repeated events by count and saturates bounded monotonic totals, and invalidates a successful Check after any board edit/undo/reset. It delegates solved/hint truth to the adapter's pure engine. It is a payload helper, not a globally registered codec; each game must bind and test its exact codec and completion evidence. `isGridEvent` validates serialized input.
- `GridPreferences` supplies the person's reader/mistake choices. Friendly consumes engine conflict indices; Plain leaves checking to the adapter; Rewind offers undo. Fill-notes controls appear only after the grown-up gate, for an explicitly chosen Grown-up reader and a registered engine callback, and default off. `GridFeedback` uses the app's preloaded tap/haptic feedback for accepted edits. An adapter can override the accepted-action callback when it has further legality checks.

No solver, save encoding, JSON parse or bank scan runs in a frame callback. No native dependency was added. No generated registries, foundation save schemas, app configuration, versions, release scripts or content approval fields were changed.

Evidence and detailed acceptance status: sibling artifacts `v4/g1-cove/REPORT.md` and `FIX-REPORT.md`. Native iOS 18.5/26.5, real iPad 60 Hz/thermal, touch-repeat tuning, VoiceOver, and maximum boards of future adapters remain required integration/device acceptance. Node/browser timing does not pass those gates.

## Cove integration work still open

TD-02 Tide Table selects three available shelf genres, filters hidden refs first, and keeps Monday gentler. Its refs are ordinary open pack entries, with no expiry or daily record.

Family Ladder: OPEN. The landscape spec defines this as the **same Word Ladder** for everyone with each person's actual route shown after solving, not a list of their latest games. G6 must expose validated route/reveal presentation from its Word Ladder adapter and retain those routes after atomic completion; G1 then mounts the comparison view. Required acceptance: two different valid routes for the same immutable ref, owner isolation, force-close/reopen, quiet per-person reveal without ranking, phone/iPad interaction. The current save keeps solution hashes, which cannot reconstruct word routes. No route data or completed view is invented here.

Island Postcard: OPEN. The Picture Grid adapter/art must provide the day's original place/picture and a validated finished-picture reveal for the album. G4 supplies that presentation; G1 mounts the postcard/gallery. Required acceptance: solve any day's registered ref, picture retained in the album in any order, no expiry, hidden-new suggestions excluded while saved work remains available, native phone/iPad reveal. The existing generic finished-ref album is implemented; it is not accepted as the picture-backed postcard view.

These two views remain unaccepted portions of TD-02 until those adapter contracts and native journeys pass. This records their dependency and ownership; it does not cancel either requirement. The independent fix review explicitly allowed formal deferral of these two adapter-dependent views. Edge input is separately open under G2 in ../puzzles/grid/README.md.
