# Privacy and family preview

Little Island has no accounts, advertising, analytics, purchases, chat, or application server. Game content, sounds, and word lists are bundled. Core game play does not send data off the device.

Game progress, options, settings, and custom family words are saved locally. Backups include this data; sharing a backup uses the system share sheet or a browser download at the grown-up's request. Keep backups private. Uninstalling the app or clearing browser storage can remove local progress. Recovery snapshots remain local.

The family preview's English speech was synthesized locally with the Mac's installed Samantha voice. Before public distribution, replace with permission-cleared human recordings or verify an appropriate distribution license. Original vector artwork and generated feedback tones are repository-owned. The Expo template license remains in LICENSE.

Public App Store metadata/privacy answers, Kids Category review, and actual family device acceptance have not been established by writing this document. TestFlight excludes children under 13; use a signed registered-device build for family trials.
