# Privacy and family preview

Little Island has no accounts, advertising, analytics, purchases, chat, live AI, or application server. Solo games, shared-device games, Explore topics, illustrations and narration are bundled and work without internet. Microphone recording is not included.

Picnic Mystery can connect nearby iPhone/iPad family devices after a grown-up creates a room and approves each guest. The local connection carries player names, positions, tasks, game events and each recipient's own role and clues. The trusted host holds the complete round in memory; guests receive recipient-specific views. Discovery exposes the room and player display name to nearby devices. Local Network permission is used for these rooms; declining it leaves offline games and Explore available. There is no public matchmaking or cloud relay.

Progress, options, settings, Explore favorites, custom family words and saved solo/shared-device roles stay in local storage. Nearby room PINs and rejoin credentials stay in memory and are excluded from saves and backups. Backups can include family names and secret game roles, so keep them private. Only a grown-up action opens the system share sheet or browser download; the app does not automatically share backups. Uninstalling the app or clearing browser storage can remove progress. Recovery snapshots remain local.

Explore reference URLs are bundled attribution text, not an unrestricted web browser. The explanations and vector illustrations are original Little Island material. The preview's English speech was synthesized locally with the Mac's installed Samantha voice. Public distribution still requires permission-cleared recordings or verification of appropriate distribution rights. Original feedback tones are repository-owned; the Expo template license remains in LICENSE.

This delivery is limited to Leon's existing adult internal tester account. Public App Store metadata, privacy answers, Kids Category review and physical family acceptance remain separate gates; this document does not establish those approvals.
