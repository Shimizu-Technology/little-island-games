File types
Each folder has different types of files, including:
ebook
: a full PDF ebook and individual image files
print-ready
: files you can share with a printer to produce your own copies (the cover and interior pages are separate tiles)
working
: this folder includes design files, fonts, text and translations

Re-share, re-mix and re-print!
All Book Dash books are published under a
Creative Commons CC BY 4.0
license. This means anyone can freely read, download, print, adapt and share Book Dash books. You can use these files to create new translations, adapt a book to your context, or plan a print run.
Research shows that physical books are optimal for young children's development. We particularly encourage you to consider printing copies for children to own, or motivating for the budget to do so. You can use our
research
and
book printing quote request document
to support you, or write to team@bookdash.org.

Partnering for impact
When people and organisations re-share Book Dash books, we get closer to the vision that every child should own a hundred books by the age of five. You become a partner in growing our impact. Please provide your details when prompted, we'd love to hear from you.
